1. Read [The Evidence Ledger](README.md).

## Opening gate

The campaign may move from `CLOSED` to `OPEN` only after all of the following exist:

- an approved HTTPS verification service and final public URL;
- an offline-held signing secret and matching pinned Ed25519 public key;
- request-body log suppression, rate limiting, retention policy, and abuse review;
- accepted and rejected submission tests against the deployed service;
- a solution-leak audit of the exact client package;
- independent Windows UAT and clean-machine qualification;
- an immutable ZIP checksum, qualification record, and human release approval;
- scheduled public and tier-specific Patreon material; and
- an owner-approved opening date and closing policy.
