# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they improve reproducibility, correct the public record, add a well-scoped experiment, or document a meaningful negative result.

## Research submissions

A submission must include:

- A precise claim or question
- Input provenance and hashes
- Complete parameters and material choices
- Reproduction instructions
- Raw output or a justified public subset
- Controls and limitations
- Related prior work
- The requested evidence status

Do not submit private correspondence, credentials, personal data, copyrighted source material without permission, or research that is not approved for public disclosure.

An interesting output will normally enter review as `EXPERIMENTAL` or `PROVISIONAL`; it does not become `VERIFIED` by assertion.
