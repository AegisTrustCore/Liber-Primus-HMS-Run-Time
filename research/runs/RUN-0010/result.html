INTERPRETATION

On solved LP1, the dollar boundary is supported as an operation-scope boundary. This motivates preserving operation state across internal records, but does not identify the operation selector for unsolved segments.

LIMITATIONS

  • The result identifies where known operations change, not which unknown operation applies to an LP2 segment.
  • Transfer of the frame model from LP1 to unsolved LP2 requires separate tests.
