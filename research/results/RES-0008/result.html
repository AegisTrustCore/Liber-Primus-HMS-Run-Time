RES-0008

Default-key OutGuess does not separate an LP2 page

PUBLISHED
BOUNDED NEGATIVEPARTIAL

CLAIM

Across the declared 75 canonical carriers, the frozen default-key OutGuess 0.13 test recognized all ten known LP1 positive controls but no LP2 page separated from the repeated FFFFFFFF false-header distribution or its page-specific matched null.

WHY IT MATTERS

It converts visually persuasive complete LP2 extractions into a controlled branch closure, preventing capacity-bound artifacts from being reused as payload evidence.

WHAT SUPPORTS IT

RUN-0008

CONTROLS

  • Ten known LP1 positive carriers, including held-out Page 08.
  • Six frozen LP2 false controls.
  • Independent retrieval agreement on all 75 pages.
  • Page-specific matched nulls and familywise correction across 225 tests.

CONTRADICTIONS

None recorded.

LIMITATIONS

  • The claim is restricted to OutGuess 0.13 with the default-key path and the hashed canonical carriers.
  • Carrier JPEGs and coefficient bitmaps are not redistributed, so the public package verifies the complete retained ledgers rather than rerunning extraction from pixels.
  • Other keys, recompressions, crops, rotations, steganographic systems, and independently frozen methods are outside scope.

WHAT IT DOES NOT CLAIM

  • That Liber Primus contains no steganography.
  • That every possible OutGuess key or carrier transformation has been tested.
  • Any LP2 plaintext, translation, cryptographic key, locator, endpoint, or complete solve.
  • That absence of a detector positive proves absence under methods outside the frozen test.

REPRODUCTION

PARTIAL

CURRENT INTERPRETATION

Remove the tested default-key OutGuess extraction path from active LP2 key channels and retain it only as a regression and canonical-carrier provenance control.
