HMS ENDEAVOUR
RES-0010

TITLE:
Solved-LP1 dollar segments align with operation frames

STATUS:
STRUCTURAL / PARTIAL

CLAIM:
Across the fifteen rune-bearing solved LP1 pages, all seven source dollar segments are operation-homogeneous and their six boundaries exactly equal the six established operation-change gaps; one of 3,003 six-boundary placements has that property.

WHY IT MATTERS:
It supports treating the dollar boundary as an operation-scope boundary and prevents automatic cipher-state resets at every internal record or page.

SUPPORTED BY:
RUN-0010

WHAT THIS DOES NOT CLAIM:
- Any newly recovered plaintext.
- That every LP2 segment uses an LP1 operation.
- That a dollar boundary alone identifies which operation to apply.
- That internal red records have no independent semantic or type role.

LIMITATIONS:
- The claim is structural and restricted to solved LP1.
- Complete third-party LP1 transcriptions and images are not redistributed.
- The observation identifies scope boundaries, not the operation selector for an unsolved segment.

CURRENT INTERPRETATION:
Use dollar boundaries as the default operation-frame boundary in the HMS runtime while keeping operation selection and LP2 transfer as separately gated questions.
