The complete Page 05 board fails the declared direct Page 33 family

PUBLISHED
BOUNDED NEGATIVEPARTIAL

CLAIM

Under the declared 640-route family, the complete Page 05 numeric board is not supported as a direct periodic key or 25-column permutation for the 267-rune Page 33 stream: all three calibrated models failed half-transfer, solved-reference, and p <= 0.01 permutation gates.

WHY IT MATTERS

It closes one precise and attractive board-to-ciphertext interpretation while retaining narrower address, selector, checksum, orbit, and multi-input possibilities.

WHAT SUPPORTS IT

RUN-0009

  • Solved-LP1 score reference.
  • 999 seeded board permutations per model.

None recorded.

LIMITATIONS

  • The original transient E1459 scorer was unavailable and is replaced by calibrated 2-, 3-, and 4-gram models.
  • Full route replay requires third-party corpus inputs not redistributed in the package.
  • The claim is limited to the declared periodic and columnar family.

WHAT IT DOES NOT CLAIM

  • That Page 05 has no role in Liber Primus.
  • That Page 33 cannot use a board-derived selector, address, checksum, orbit, or additional input.
  • Any LP2 plaintext, key, locator, endpoint, wallet, or complete solve.
