## Boundary rule

The public roadmap states what category of work is happening and what gate remains. Private research retains unreviewed sources, exact experimental assignments, unpublished candidates, and sensitive routes until intentional promotion.

A visible source tree is not a customer release. Ordinary-user downloads require separate packaging, clean-environment testing, an exact-subject gate, and human approval under the [Distribution and User Experience Standard](docs/DISTRIBUTION_STANDARD.md).

Releases follow demonstrated capability, not arbitrary calendar promises.
