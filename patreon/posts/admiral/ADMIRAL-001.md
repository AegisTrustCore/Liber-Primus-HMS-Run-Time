# ADMIRAL-001 — The Frontier Boundary

Status: **DRAFT — NOT POSTED**

Admiral is intended for experimental collaboration, not automatic access to every internal file. Frontier releases may eventually include unstable instruments, beta solver modules, review queues, expanded compute, authenticated API/socket access, experimental exports, and an add-on SDK.

Every frontier item will still have a version, scope, instability warning, access policy, data boundary, and revocation or supersession path. Candidate hypotheses remain labeled as candidates. Vault material remains private unless separately approved.

No Admiral instrument is released today. The frontier program begins with architecture and governance so later experimental capability does not outrun evidence control or security.
