# CARTOGRAPHER-001 — How the Evidence Ledger Will Work

Status: **POSTED 2026-08-12**

Live post: https://www.patreon.com/AegisTrustCore/posts/how-evidence-001-166540550

Cartographer material will go deeper than a progress update. A complete research packet is intended to include:

- A precise claim and evidence status
- Canonical input provenance and checksums
- The full method and material parameters
- Positive, negative, and shuffled controls
- Actual outputs and failed branches
- Limitations and alternative explanations
- A structured dataset or ledger when rights permit
- The smallest conclusion supported by the run

The first packets will focus on known controls and bounded negatives, because those provide a reliable base for the later workstation and batch tools. Advanced GP, Page 32 routing, operation-bank, batch, and visual systems remain planned until the instrument registry changes status.
