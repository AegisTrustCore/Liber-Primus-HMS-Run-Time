Seventy-five leaves. One declared ledger. Five ways to test whether the carrier still matches the record.

The free HMS Corpus Manifest Verifier checks a local Liber Primus page folder against the canonical 75-page identity manifest. It reports a clean match, altered bytes, missing pages, unexpected files, and unsafe manifest paths. It can also accept selected declared pages to locate the shared folder—but it always verifies the complete manifest before it can say `PASS`.

What `PASS` means: the declared bytes match the manifest.

What `PASS` does not mean: historical authenticity, rights, transcription correctness, translation, route, or solution.
