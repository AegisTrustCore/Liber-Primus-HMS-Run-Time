# HMS Dispatch 000 — The Public Evidence Layer

Status: **DRAFT — NOT YET POSTED**

HMS Endeavour begins with a boundary.

The public GitHub repository is now being organized as the evidence and verification layer for our Liber Primus research. It does not claim that the complete corpus has been solved. It separates what can be reproduced, what is a known control, what remains structural or provisional, what failed, and what has been corrected.

The first source audit hash-inventoried 1,322 files totaling approximately 3.84 GB, including 266 ZIP archives and 11,541 archive members. The reviewed structured records did not explicitly report newly recovered plaintext, key material, a locator, or an endpoint. They did contain valuable control replays, Page 32 structural candidates, bounded negative results, and a Page 73 interpretation that must be corrected.

That is the standard HMS will follow: positive and negative evidence, published methodically and without turning an internal experiment label into a public proof claim.

## What is being built now

- Canonical corpus provenance and checksums
- Public reproduction packages for solved-material controls
- Page 32, 72, and 73 research dossiers
- The public instrument registry
- The foundation for the GP29 calculator and Liber Runtime

Tools will be released according to capability and validation status, not calendar pressure. The initial release is the foundation of a growing research station.
