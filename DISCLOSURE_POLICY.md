# Discovery Disclosure and Validation Policy

Significant discoveries are handled through a fixed evidence-preservation procedure.

1. Freeze the experiment definition, complete parameter family, inputs, and outputs.
2. Generate content hashes and a HashLock.
3. Preserve the deterministic environment manifest.
4. Rerun from clean inputs without editing the target criteria.
5. Assign an independent reviewer to reproduce the bounded claim.
6. Generate the Evidence, Claim, and ProofLock objects.
7. Assess personal-data, security, legal, redistribution, and third-party impact.
8. Decide whether an approved member preview is appropriate.
9. Pass the Public Release Gate before public publication.
10. Preserve subsequent corrections, supersessions, or retractions bidirectionally.

No discovery is announced as solved, verified, authenticated, or Cicada-originated before this procedure is complete. A Patreon tier does not bypass it.

If a discovery includes a credential, exploitable system, personal information, active private service, or dangerous instruction, pause publication and use private responsible disclosure.
