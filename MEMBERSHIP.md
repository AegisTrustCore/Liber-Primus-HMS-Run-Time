# HMS Endeavour Membership

Last verified against the live Patreon configuration: 2026-08-12

The public GitHub repository is the permanent **Observer** layer. Patreon supports continued development and progressively deeper access to the expedition's instruments, data, experiments, and research process. Membership does not purchase an answer, ownership of a result, or a promise that an unsolved page has been solved.

The Patreon creator page is live at [Aegis Trust Core](https://www.patreon.com/c/AegisTrustCore). The first controlled round of public and tier-specific posts was published on 2026-08-12.

Current and future material destinations are defined in the [Patreon release matrix](patreon/RELEASE_MATRIX.md). GitHub remains the authority for public releases; the [instrument registry](instruments/README.md) remains the authority for tool availability.

## Membership ladder

| Pilgrim | $3/month; 7-day trial | **Follow** the expedition |

Prices are the current Patreon configuration and may change only through an explicit membership decision.

## Available now and planned scope

### Pilgrim — Follow

Available now: the monthly HMS Dispatch, member development updates, GitHub and Runtime release alerts, member research polls, LP community challenges, member-only challenge hints, selected research previews, early release notes, member research discussion, and the public research timeline.

As HMS expands, Pilgrim will receive selected entry-level Runtime tools and research utilities after they are completed, tested, and released.

### Navigator — Investigate

Available now: everything in Pilgrim, plus 7–14 day early access to selected major research releases, downloadable experiment results as released, selected findings and teasers, member challenge datasets and hints, first looks at research directions, and Navigator research discussion.

As HMS expands, Runtime beta access, solver datasets, and advanced GP/Gematria tools will be added after their modules are completed, tested, and released.

### Cartographer — Research

Available now: everything in Navigator, plus complete monthly research packets, deeper experiment ledgers, bounded negative and rejected-result archives, structured research datasets as released, a monthly Cartographer Briefing, group research sessions, early challenge datasets, structured hypothesis submission, and contributor credit when qualifying work enters a public HMS release.

As HMS expands, the Cartographer workspace will add advanced Runtime modules, batch experiment tools, the operation-bank explorer, page-state and routing tools, and the visual-analysis laboratory after each module is completed, tested, and released.

### Admiral — Build and validate

Available now: everything in Cartographer, plus frontier research reports, selected unpublished hypotheses, early reproduced results, candidate validation, peer review, research collaboration, proposal submission, and experimental exports when approved packets are available.

As the developer platform comes online, Admiral access will expand to include experimental instruments, prerelease HMS builds, beta solver modules, expanded compute, the Plugin SDK, HMS API access, research-branch previews, developer tooling, and advanced collaboration systems after each capability is completed, tested, and released.

Vault access is never automatic.

## Release principle

HMS Endeavour is being built in public and released progressively. GitHub will continue to grow with free tools, documentation, public research, verification systems, and stable public releases. Patreon members receive progressively deeper access to the HMS Research Station, datasets, research builds, and advanced tools as they are completed and validated.

Not every HMS working file, dataset, tool, or research candidate will be released publicly. Approved public truth claims are not permanently withheld to manufacture scarcity, and a result does not become more true because it appears in a higher tier.

Every benefit that names a tool or capability is governed by its visible status: `PLANNED`, `IN DEVELOPMENT`, `INTERNAL TESTING`, `EXPERIMENTAL`, `BETA`, `STABLE`, or `RELEASED`.
