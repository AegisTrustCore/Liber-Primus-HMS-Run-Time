# HMS Endeavour Membership

Last verified against the live Patreon configuration: 2026-08-13

The public GitHub repository is the permanent **Observer** layer. Patreon supports continued development and progressively deeper access to the expedition's instruments, data, experiments, and research process. Membership does not purchase an answer, ownership of a result, or a promise that an unsolved page has been solved.

The Patreon creator page is live at [Aegis Trust Core](https://www.patreon.com/c/AegisTrustCore). The first controlled round of public and tier-specific posts was published on 2026-08-12, and [HMS Status 001](https://www.patreon.com/AegisTrustCore/posts/hms-status-001-166550733) was published on 2026-08-13.

Current and future material destinations are defined in the [Patreon release matrix](patreon/RELEASE_MATRIX.md). GitHub remains the authority for public releases; the [instrument registry](instruments/README.md) remains the authority for tool availability.

GitHub may record that a member transmission exists in the [metadata-only Patreon manifest](patreon/public-manifest.json). Its full post body, gated clues, private attachments, and production material remain on Patreon or in private HMS staging.

## Available now and planned scope

### Pilgrim — Follow

Available now: published HMS Dispatches and status posts, GitHub release alerts, selected research previews, early release notes, member discussion, and the public research timeline.

Campaign-based: member-only challenge hints and LP community challenge material are delivered only when a specific Expedition is formally opened. Expedition 001 is currently closed.

No GP29, Runtime, or solver download is currently included. As HMS expands, Pilgrim will receive selected entry-level tools only after they are completed, tested, and released.

### Navigator — Investigate

Available now: everything currently published for Pilgrim, plus Navigator-level research previews, discussion, and release notices.

Campaign-based: curated datasets, stronger hints, and solution-path material are released only during an opened Expedition and according to its release gate. Expedition 001 is currently closed.

GP29, Liber Runtime beta, solver datasets, advanced GP/Gematria tools, and Navigator downloads are not currently available. They will be added only after completion, testing, and formal release.

### Cartographer — Research

Available now: everything currently published for Navigator, plus Cartographer-level briefings, research-packet structure, and release notices.

Campaign-based: worksheets, hypothesis packets, structured datasets, deeper ledgers, and bounded-negative material are delivered only when approved and released for a specific campaign. Expedition 001 is closed; its worksheet package is staged, not live.

Advanced Runtime access, batch experimentation, operation banks, routing tools, and visual research labs are not currently available. Access begins only after those instruments are completed, tested, and formally released.

### Admiral — Build and validate

Available now: everything currently published for Cartographer, plus the Frontier Boundary briefing, collaboration policy, and Admiral-level release notices.

Campaign and frontier releases: validation packets, selected hypotheses, reproducibility material, and data exports appear only after review and an explicit release decision.

No experimental instrument, prerelease HMS build, advanced solver, API access, compute allocation, or Plugin SDK is available today. Such access begins only after the relevant system is built, tested, and formally released.

Vault access is never automatic.

## Release principle

## First-month delivery commitment

The first month is organized as four weekly releases rather than one vague promise:

| Tier | Concrete month-one value |
|---|---|
| Pilgrim | Four guided dispatches: public-deck orientation, 75-page reading route, Expedition orientation when open, and a month-end ship’s log |
| Navigator | Four analytical labs: evidence classification, page-provenance comparison, stronger campaign analysis or a synthetic substitute, and a bounded-negative research queue |
| Cartographer | Four reusable research packets: reproduction ledger, manifest audit, Expedition worksheet or methods workbook, and a reproduction debrief template |
| Admiral | Four validation packets: release-candidate UAT, independent package qualification, Expedition privacy/service review when deployable, and a month-end build decision ledger |

If Expedition 001 remains closed, its week becomes a synthetic methods lab; Patreon will not imply that a campaign benefit exists when its release gate has not passed. Downloadable member builds require the same version, checksum, limitations, malware scan, and approval discipline as public tools.

HMS Endeavour is being built in public and released progressively. GitHub will continue to grow with free tools, documentation, public research, verification systems, and stable public releases. Patreon members receive progressively deeper access to the HMS Research Station, datasets, research builds, and advanced tools as they are completed and validated.

Not every HMS working file, dataset, tool, or research candidate will be released publicly. Approved public truth claims are not permanently withheld to manufacture scarcity, and a result does not become more true because it appears in a higher tier.

Every benefit that names a tool or capability is governed by its visible status: `PLANNED`, `IN_DEVELOPMENT`, `INTERNAL_TESTING`, `RELEASE_CANDIDATE`, `RELEASED`, `DEPRECATED`, or `RETIRED`. Development, experimental, beta, release-candidate, and stable channels are recorded separately. The target access matrix in the [HMS Product Ladder](docs/HMS_PRODUCT_LADDER.md) is not a claim that an unreleased capability is available.
