# HMS Endeavour Membership

Last verified against the Patreon configuration: 2026-08-12

The public GitHub repository is the permanent **Observer** layer. Patreon supports continued development and progressively more capable member environments. Membership purchases access, organization, computation, participation, and development support—not ownership of a result and not a promise that an unsolved page has been solved.

The Patreon creator page is live at [Aegis Trust Core](https://www.patreon.com/c/AegisTrustCore). The first controlled round of public and tier-specific posts was published on 2026-08-12.

Launch history and remaining presentation work are tracked in the [Patreon pre-launch checklist](patreon/PRELAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md).

Current and future material destinations are defined in the [Patreon release matrix](patreon/RELEASE_MATRIX.md). GitHub remains the authority for public releases; the instrument registry remains the authority for tool availability.

## What is configured now

### Pilgrim — Enter the Expedition

- Monthly HMS Dispatch
- GitHub release alerts
- Public LP challenges
- Community posts and polls
- Public research timeline
- Announcements when Runtime tools release

### Navigator — Follow the Hidden Routes

Navigator includes the Pilgrim communication benefits plus configured early access to major findings, downloadable experiment results, research polls, early release notes, challenge hints, datasets, member discussions, and previews.

Runtime beta and advanced GP/Gematria tooling are **planned capabilities**, not completed launch claims.

### Cartographer — Enter the Laboratory

Cartographer includes Navigator scope plus planned complete research packets, deeper experiment ledgers, negative-result archives, structured datasets, briefings, group research sessions, hypothesis submission, and contributor credit where work enters a public release.

Advanced Runtime modules, batch tools, operation-bank exploration, Page 32/state-routing tools, and visual-analysis modules remain **planned** until listed otherwise in the [instrument registry](instruments/README.md).

### Admiral — Frontier Research

Admiral is configured for future experimental instruments, beta solvers, collaboration, proposal and review workflows, expanded compute, API access, experimental exports, a plugin SDK, and candidate validation.

These are a **planned frontier scope**, not a representation that every listed entitlement exists today. Vault material does not automatically become Admiral material.

## Release principle

Every benefit that names a tool or capability is governed by its visible status: `PLANNED`, `IN DEVELOPMENT`, `INTERNAL TESTING`, `EXPERIMENTAL`, `BETA`, `STABLE`, or `RELEASED`.

Members support both current access and continued development. Releases follow demonstrated capability, not arbitrary calendar promises. Public truth claims remain public once approved; higher tiers may receive earlier access, additional computation, private workspaces, or advanced tooling.
